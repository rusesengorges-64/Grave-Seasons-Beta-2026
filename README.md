## 🕹️ About

Grave Seasons is an isometric pixel-art murder-mystery farming and life sim set in the unsettling mountain town of Ashenridge. Play as an escaped convict, build a new life, investigate supernatural killings, and make choices that shape the fate of the town and its residents. Join the beta test, help us refine the experience, and receive a free game key for participating.

Grave Seasons is for players who enjoy cozy farming sims, character-driven mysteries, supernatural horror, romance, and replayable narrative choices.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🌾 **Seasonal Farming** — Grow crops across four changing seasons using specialized farm plots.
- 🔍 **Murder Mystery** — Investigate supernatural killings and uncover the secrets hidden in Ashenridge.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Living Community** — Meet more than 30 residents with individual stories, schedules, and secrets.
- ❤️ **Romance and Relationships** — Build relationships and romance potential partners, including dangerous suspects.
- 🛠️ **Crafting and Upgrades** — Craft useful recipes, occult items, tools, and improvements for your farm.
- 🌙 **Day and Night Exploration** — Explore Ashenridge around the clock as nighttime reveals new dangers and opportunities.
- 🎲 **Dynamic Killer Campaigns** — Each playthrough can feature a different supernatural killer, motive, target, and story.
- ⛏️ **Explore Ashenridge** — Farm, forage, fish, mine, investigate, and explore diverse locations across the town.
